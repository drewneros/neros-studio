How to take model digitals at home

By Drew NerosModel since 2015, photographer

You can take model digitals at home with a phone and a window. Most models do. And most of them never hear back, because their digitals look like a photoshoot.

Why your digitals get ignored

An agent opens your email and decides in seconds. They aren't looking for your best photo. They're looking for a reason to say no, and a home shoot gives them plenty: the wrong light, the wrong lens, the wrong face.

The frustrating part is that none of it is about your looks. It's the setup. And nobody tells you the setup, because the people who know it are on the other side of the table.

What the agency standard looks like

I've been on both sides since 2015, as a model with agencies in many countries and as the photographer shooting digitals for agency boards. The standard is strict, it's specific, and you can hit it in your living room once you know it.

There's one camera mistake that gets models rejected before an agent even looks at their face. Most people make it on their first frame.
