How long do modeling agencies take to respond?

By Drew NerosModel since 2015, photographer

Usually, they don't. Agencies reply to the models they want and stay silent with everyone else. So the real question is why they didn't reply to you.

Silence is an answer

It just never tells you what was wrong. Was it the photos? The agency? The email? Most models guess, resend the same digitals to the same agencies a month later, and get the same nothing.

Having been on both sides since 2015, I can tell you the decision rarely comes down to the face. It's usually something small the model could have fixed in a day.

Before you follow up

There's a right time to follow up and a way to do it that keeps you on the agent's list. There's also a way that quietly takes you off it. Most models pick the second one.
