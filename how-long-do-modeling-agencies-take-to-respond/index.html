How long do modeling agencies take to respond?

By Drew NerosModel since 2015, photographer

Usually they don't respond at all. Agencies get far more submissions than they can answer, so they reply to the people they want and stay silent with everyone else. Silence is an answer. It just never tells you why.

The timeline

Day 0Send, and write the date down.
Week 1 to 2Nothing. No follow up, no resend, no DM.
Week 2 to 4One follow up. Reply to your first email so they see the whole thread.
Week 4 to 6Still nothing? Take it as a no and look hard at your digitals.

Send the follow up once, never twice. If you resubmit to the same agency later, do it with new photos and wait at least three months from the first email.

What an agent checks, in order

  1. Format. Is this a digital or a photoshoot? A photoshoot reads as hiding something.
  2. Proportions. Height and build, from the full-length front and profile.
  3. Face. Bone structure and skin, from an unretouched close-up.
  4. Fit. Does this person fit the board right now?

Check the agency before you blame your photos

A perfect digital sent to the wrong agency is still a no. Open their board for your gender and find three models with your height and look. If you can't find them, it's the wrong agency. Also check they sign people from your city or country.

Fashion boards usually start around 5′8″ (173 cm) for women and 5′11″ (180 cm) for men. Shorter models do get signed, but only with a face that stops the agent. Commercial boards take a wider height range and want a relatable face and a real smile.

What goes in the submission

If the agency has a form on its website, use the form. Email only when there isn't one. Either way, include your first and last name, real age, height in cm and feet, measurements, city and country, all six digitals, your Instagram if it's mostly you, and a short intro and walk video.

Make the subject line something an agent can sort without opening it: New face submission / Your Name / 178 cm / City.

Leave out photoshoot images, links that ask for a login, huge files, and your life story. Three lines is enough. And never send to twenty agencies in one email with everyone in CC.
