What to wear to a model casting

By Drew NerosModel since 2015, photographer

They decide before you speak. Sometimes before you reach the middle of the room. Your outfit is part of that decision, and most models dress for the wrong job.

You're not being judged. You're being filtered.

Twenty people get called for the same brief. The client needs three. They keep the ones they can place instantly and let everyone else go, often for reasons nobody says out loud.

When a client says you looked "off", they mean something specific. It's almost always fixable, and it's almost never your face.
