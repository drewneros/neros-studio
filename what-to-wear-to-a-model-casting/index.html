What to wear to a model casting

By Drew NerosModel since 2015, photographer

Clients often decide before you've reached the middle of the room. What you're wearing is part of that, and a lot of models show up dressed for a different job.

Twenty called, three kept

Twenty people get called for the same brief and the client needs three. They keep the ones they can picture in the job straight away, and nobody explains the rest.

If you've ever heard that you looked "off", that usually means one of a few specific things. Most of them you can fix in the corridor before you go in.

What to bring to a modeling casting call

A few things are worth having in your bag, and there's one thing you should never wear in. I learned most of this by getting it wrong at my own castings, starting in 2015.
