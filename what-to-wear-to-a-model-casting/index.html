Guides for models

What to wear to a model casting

By Drew NerosModel since 2015, photographer

Your outfit isn't there to make you look good. It's there so they can place you in one look. At a casting you are one of twenty people called for the same brief, and the one they can read fastest usually gets the job.

The default casting outfit

TopFitted and plain. No logo, print or slogan.
BottomsStraight or slim denim, or plain black trousers.
ShoesSomething that shows your leg line. Keep heels in your bag until they ask.
ShowAnkles, wrists and neck, so they can read your proportions.
HairDown, and the same as in your digitals.
FaceClean, or as close to it as you're willing to go.
AvoidAnything that rustles, jingles or needs fixing.
LayerOne you can take off if the room runs warm.

Why styling loses castings

The wrong outfit puts you in the wrong category before you speak. If they can't read your type in one look, they move on to someone they can. Clean lines and a clear silhouette make the decision easy for them.

The other way styling costs you: your digitals promised a different person. Different hair, different proportions, different look. Trust breaks the moment you walk in, and trust is what they are buying. Turn up looking like your digitals.

What to bring

  • Heels, if the brief might need them.
  • That one removable layer.
  • Your phone with your digitals and book ready to show, in case they ask.

In the room

They decide fast, often before you reach the middle of the room. Keep your face relaxed rather than expressive. Keep your shoulders soft and your weight on both feet. Keep still: fidgeting reads as uncertainty. And walk the way you'd walk to a train you're not late for. A rehearsed entrance works against you.

Arrive early enough to stand still for ten minutes before you go in. It's the only part of a casting you fully control.
